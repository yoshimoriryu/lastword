how to play

goal

Last one standing wins. Words pile onto your board; your HP bar is the free space left. Board full = dead.

typing

Type the first word of your board. When it turns green, press space to commit it. Typos must be fixed — backspace deletes one letter, cmd/ctrl+backspace wipes the word. Pressing space too early is itself a typo.

attack

Every committed word charges your attack. Longer words and faster typing charge more; whole attack-words launch at opponents when the charge crosses 1. Attack words land red on their board.

word lengthfast*normalslow

*fast = under half the baseline pace (baseline ≈ 50 wpm). Values shown before the combo multiplier.

combo & surge

Commit words back-to-back (within s, no typos) to build a streak. The streak multiplies your attack:

streakmultiplier

From streak the ⚡ surge meter charges (max ). When your streak breaks — typo or idling — the whole charge fires at your opponents as one burst. Big combo, big bomb.

Or press enter to fire it manually: perfect timing, no accuracy hit — but it resets your streak, same as a break. Hold for a bigger bomb or dump it while your opponent is already drowning; your call.

defense

Incoming attacks telegraph for s on the red gauge beside your board before landing. Attack you send during that window cancels incoming words first — keep typing under fire.

modes

surge — everything above. auto-surge — surge fires only on streak break, no manual enter. classic — no surge at all; combos multiply harder instead (up to ×2.5). Host picks in the lobby.

pace

The neutral word stream speeds up forever. Nobody outlives it — matches always end.
